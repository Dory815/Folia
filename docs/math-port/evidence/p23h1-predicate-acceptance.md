## Regressions and limits

Copy (146 / three / 17 mutants), constructor (91 / two / six), append (62 / one /
five), and sparse factory binding (116 / ten / one) passed after predicate delivery.

baseline with its Null-message mutant and factory's full 116-case baseline/graph

body logic stayed identical. No full-core success is inferred from focused graphs.

return invalid text types, arbitrary source field types and every callback graph
